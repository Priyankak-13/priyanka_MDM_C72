About the System

Why reasoning in degrees beats a hard line

What is fuzzy logic?

Classical software checks "is HR > 100?" and gets a flat yes or no. Fuzzy logic instead asks how much a value belongs to a category. A heart rate of 98 bpm isn't purely "normal" or purely "high" — it can be 70% normal and 30% high at the same time. That partial membership is captured with membership functions (triangles and trapezoids) instead of a single threshold.

Why not just use thresholds?

A hard threshold treats 99 bpm and 101 bpm as if they belong to completely different worlds, even though they're clinically almost identical. It also can't blend several vitals gracefully — a patient who is borderline on two readings at once gets no credit for that overlap. Fuzzy logic captures exactly this kind of borderline, multi-factor reasoning, closer to how a clinician actually thinks.

How this system works

Every submitted reading passes through four hand-coded stages: fuzzification (crisp numbers become membership degrees), the rule base (12 IF–THEN clinical rules), inference (rules combined using minimum for AND, maximum for OR), and centroid defuzzification (the fuzzy result becomes one crisp 0–100 risk score). No fuzzy-logic library is used anywhere — the entire pipeline is plain Python and NumPy array math.

Educational disclaimer: Fuzzy-CDSS is a college mini-project built to demonstrate fuzzy inference. It is not a certified medical device and must never be used to diagnose, treat, or make real decisions about any patient. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

    Under the Hood

    How the fuzzy logic system works

    01

    Fuzzification

    Each crisp reading (e.g. HR = 98 bpm) is converted into membership degrees for Low / Normal / High using triangular and trapezoidal functions.

    02

    Rule Base

    12 IF–THEN clinical rules relate combinations of Heart Rate, BP, and SpO₂ to a risk outcome: Low, Medium, or High.

    03

    Inference

    Rules combine their inputs with minimum for AND and maximum for OR, then rules sharing an outcome are aggregated with maximum.

    04

    Defuzzification

    The aggregated fuzzy risk shape is converted back into one crisp number using the centroid (center of gravity) method.
